why choose vedic astrology from nepal

nepal is the birthplace of some of the world's most revered vedic traditions. our astrologers are trained in classical jyotish, the ancient system of vedic astrology that uses precise astronomical calculations to read your birth chart — your janam kundali. unlike western astrology, vedic jyotish accounts for the precession of the equinoxes, giving more accurate planetary positions and life predictions.

our consultations cover kundali reading, rashifal (yearly predictions), kundali matching for marriage, vastu shastra for home and business, numerology, and career and finance guidance — all rooted in the nepali and himalayan vedic tradition. explore nepali astrology predictions in our blog or book a nepali astrologer online consultation.
